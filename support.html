Subscriptions & billing

3toGo Plus is $6.99/month or $39.99/year, each with a 3-day free trial, billed by Apple. Manage or cancel any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → 3toGo Plus. Cancel during the trial and you are never charged. On a new device, tap Restore purchases on the plan screen.

Unlocking your apps

Open 3toGo and answer the 3-question gate to unlock your blocked apps for your chosen break length. Need everything open right now? In 3toGo: Settings → Open everything now opens every blocked app for 30 minutes. It is free, you get four a month, and your streak is not affected. Want a longer break from blocking? Settings → Take a break pauses it for 1 to 14 days with no questions first, and you can end it early at any time.

Screen Time permission

Blocking uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) with your permission. If blocking is not working, check iOS Settings → Screen Time is enabled and that 3toGo has permission; you can re-grant it from 3toGo's Settings tab.
