3toGo Support

3toGo blocks the apps you choose behind three quick Spanish questions. Answer them, earn your unlock, and turn scrolling into learning.

Contact

Email tanvir@3togo.app. Include your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when something went wrong. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Subscriptions & billing

3toGo Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, billed by Apple. New subscribers start with a 3-day free trial. The exact price is shown in the app and on the App Store sheet before you confirm. Manage or cancel any time in iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, 3toGo, or in 3toGo under Settings, 3toGo Plus, Manage in the App Store. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged. On a new device, tap Restore purchases on the plan screen or in 3toGo's Settings.

Have a code? On the plan screen, tap Redeem a code.

Unlocking your apps

Open 3toGo and answer the 3-question gate to unlock your blocked apps for your chosen break length. When a blocked app shows the 3toGo screen, tap Answer in 3toGo. On iOS 26.5 or later that opens 3toGo straight away. On older iOS versions, tap the notification that follows (notifications need to be on). If nothing happens, open 3toGo yourself. If the questions don't start, tap Answer 3 to unlock on Home.

Need everything open right now? In 3toGo, Settings, Emergency Unlock opens every blocked app for 30 minutes and never costs you your streak. You get three free a month; after that it costs 100 XP, or five quick questions if your balance is short. The same control is on the Home Screen: press and hold the 3toGo icon. Want a longer break from blocking? Settings, Vacation Mode pauses it for 1, 3, 7 or 14 days. You answer a short question set up front (5 questions for 1 day, 12 for 3 days, 20 for 7 days, 30 for 14 days) and can end the break early at any time.

Screen Time permission

Blocking uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) with your permission. If blocking is not working, check that iOS Settings, Screen Time is enabled and that 3toGo has permission; you can re-grant it from 3toGo's Settings tab.

Privacy

Your answers, your progress and the apps you block stay on your iPhone. To measure our ads, 3toGo uses Meta's SDK. It tells Meta when the app is installed or opened and how long you used it, when you finish the intro questions, when the plan screen is shown, when a trial starts, and when a subscription is paid, with the price. Meta also gets basic device details like model, iOS version and IP address, and diagnostic data such as crash reports.

Once you have an active trial or subscription, 3toGo shows Apple's tracking prompt once. If you allow it, Meta also gets your device's advertising identifier. You can change your answer any time in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
