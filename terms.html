The service

3toGo lets you block apps you choose and unlock them for limited windows by answering learning questions. It is a self-management tool for your own device and is not a parental-control or surveillance product.

Subscriptions

3toGo Plus is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple account. You need an active subscription or free trial to use 3toGo once the intro is done. There's a monthly plan and a yearly plan. If your Apple ID has never had a 3toGo Plus subscription or free trial, your plan starts with a 3-day free trial. Prices are shown in the app before purchase.

Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm the purchase, or when the free trial ends if you started one. Your subscription renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and Apple charges the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends. To avoid being charged after a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple account settings. In 3toGo, Settings, 3toGo Plus, Manage in the App Store takes you there.

If you switch to a different plan during your free trial, the rest of the trial can end when the new plan starts. Apple handles refund requests for App Store purchases. If your subscription ends, 3toGo turns blocking off when you next open it and reach the plan screen. The apps you picked stay saved on your iPhone, and blocking comes back when you subscribe again.
