3toGo — Terms of Use

Effective: June 13, 2026

3toGo is a screen-time management and language-learning app for your own device, offered by Tanvir Singh Manku (Micro Habit Labs).

The service

3toGo lets you block apps you choose and unlock them for limited windows by answering learning questions. It is a self-management tool for your own device and is not a parental-control or surveillance product.

Subscriptions

3toGo Plus is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple account. It renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple account settings. Prices are shown in the app before purchase.

Acceptable use

You agree to use 3toGo only to manage your own device and not to attempt to circumvent, reverse-engineer, or misuse the service.

Disclaimer

3toGo is provided "as is" without warranties. Blocking depends on Apple's Screen Time system and may be affected by iOS behavior outside our control.
