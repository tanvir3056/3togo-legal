3toGo — Privacy Policy

Effective: June 13, 2026

3toGo helps you manage your own screen time by asking short language-learning questions before unlocking apps you choose to block. Privacy is a design principle of the product: your data stays on your device.

Screen Time data

3toGo uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) frameworks with your explicit permission, solely to block and unblock the apps you select on your own device. The identifiers of your selected apps and all related usage state never leave your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share Screen Time data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or measurement of any kind.

Learning data

Your question history, streaks, points, and progress are stored locally on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Analytics and tracking

3toGo contains no third-party analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no trackers.

Your choices

You can revoke Screen Time permission at any time in iOS Settings, and deleting the app deletes its on-device data.
