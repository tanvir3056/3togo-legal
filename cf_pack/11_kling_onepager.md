# The Kling Co-Launch Playbook (one-pager)

Prepared by Tanvir Singh Manku. The page Roemie gets if he says "send it". Keep it to this length; it is a proposal to react to, not a report.

---

## The idea in one line

Day one access to a new model is commoditised: Freepik, fal and Krea all have it, and they all post the same demo reels. The one co-marketing asset no other platform can give Kling is physical proof: real things, made with their model, in the first week. Build the launch campaign around that difference.

## Why this works

- Model labs choose launch partners for the adoption story they generate. Every aggregator offers screens; only CF's users cut, print, stitch and film. A wall of physical makes in week one is evidence no demo reel matches.
- It is the Netflix logic from CF's own history (attention and a headline for both sides, "knit your own Demogorgon") pointed at model labs instead of brands.
- CF already implements new models within a day, so the delta is not engineering; it is the wrapper and the tracking. The playbook then reruns for every Google, ByteDance and Kling drop. Build once, run monthly.

## The mechanic (CF side only; what any partner adds is a negotiation, not an assumption)

1. Template pack built per capability, not per date: three pre-made variants (longer clips: "your make-along in one clip"; audio: "narrated maker story"; image or motion: "bring your design to life"). Launch day only needs a headline swap, so a slipped provider date costs nothing.
2. One landing page: challenge brief, gallery, sign-up. Every channel post, email and in-product tile points at it.
3. Launch hour: model live in Studio, page opens, channels push, email goes.
4. Days 2 to 14: daily featured makes on CF channels; winners get Studio subscriptions.
5. Entries require an account and a generation, so participation is a tracked sign-up plus activation in one step.

## Effort (bare bones)

Landing page ~2 days. Template pack ~3 days, reusable. Post kit ~1.5 days. In-product tile is config. Total: about one week of the content team, no engineering beyond the page.

## Measurement

- Attribution: the landing page, UTMs on every link, a campaign tag on the in-product entry, "where did you hear about this" at sign-up as the check.
- Target: 20,000 tracked users in 14 days for a feature scale push, with 50%+ making a first generation in week one.
- Secondary: generations on the new model, challenge posts, D7 retention of the cohort against paid cohorts, subscriber conversion at day 30.
- Kill line: under 5,000 sign-ups or activation under 25% means the mechanic gets redesigned before the next launch.

## What I would explore with the partner (asks, not assumptions)

Early access under embargo, a named mention in their launch post, reposts of the best community makes, credits for prizes. Each is a negotiation; the campaign runs and gets measured even if the answer to all four is no.

## Risks

Provider dates slip (assets are date-portable by design). Moderation load in launch week (moderated tier, human review before featuring). Novelty decay (why the KPI is cohort quality, not just the spike).
