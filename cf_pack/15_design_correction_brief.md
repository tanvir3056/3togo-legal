# PAGE 2

## Navigation

**01 · MODEL PARTNERS**

## Heading

# Turn model launches into consumer campaigns

## Proposition

When a new model lands in Studio, make the launch about what CF's community can create with it, not the fact that the model is available.

## The insight

Freepik, fal, Krea and other platforms can expose the same models quickly. Availability alone is difficult to own.

Creative Fabrica has a different asset: a community already making physical and digital things.

A model launch becomes more distinctive when the source material is a quilt, product photo, crochet character, printable or design made by a real user.

## Activation

### Bring Your Make To Life

**1 · Make**  
Upload something you made or sell.

**2 · Animate**  
Use the newly released model to bring it to life.

**3 · Share**  
Publish the before-and-after result.

Examples:

**Knitted dragon → breathes fire**  
**Handmade illustration → moves**  
**Seller product photo → short promotional clip**

## Why the partner says yes

- Generation volume during the launch window
- Consumer-created examples that look different from standard AI demos
- A distribution story built around real makers
- A reusable launch format rather than a one-off integration announcement

## Why CF says yes

**The model launch becomes an acquisition event.**

Require an account and a first generation to participate. Attribute the campaign through one landing page.

### Primary measures

**Signups · first generations · share rate · 14-day return**

Use the first campaign as the baseline. The second campaign must beat it.

## Why it scales

### Build the activation kit once

**Landing page template → Prompt pack → Example format → Tracking → Moderation rules**

Then change the model and creative examples for each release.

A slipped launch date does not kill the work because the assets are built around capability, not a calendar date.
