## The brand activation picture in one page

- Why brands pay: earned reach (Heinz A.I. Ketchup: 1.15B earned impressions, MEDIUM), UGC volume as media (a TikTok Branded Mission drew 1.5M user videos for one brand), cultural relevance without "AI slop" risk (post-backlash, brands need consumers as the creators and a physical object as the output), launch headlines, first-party data.

## The creative-volume picture in one page

- Structural demand: Meta Andromeda (Dec 2024 engineering post: 10,000x model capacity, +6% recall, +8% ads quality) makes each genuinely distinct creative its own retrieval entry point; near-duplicates collapse. Practitioner test: 25 diverse creatives in one ad set beat 5x5: +17% conversions, -16% cost (MEDIUM). Advantage+ generative features push variant counts further.
- TikTok: 5 to 7 assets per ad group, refreshed at least weekly, associated with ~1.5x ROAS (MEDIUM; re-verify exact wording); Symphony auto-generates creatives.
- Google PMax: up to 15 headlines, 5 descriptions, 20 images, 5 videos per asset group; ~40+ assets for a "complete" group (HIGH).
- Who serves it: ad-creative specialists (AdCreative.ai now Appier-owned and on Shopify App Store; Creatify; Omneky; Icon; Arcads; Poolday) via commerce/ads surfaces; suites (Canva, Freepik, Envato) via bundling and affiliates. Almost nobody owns the POD/marketplace-seller niche end to end: CF's wedge.
- CF's real assets for the lane: 34-model API, Studio tools, 15M+ asset marketplace (asset-count unverified), a registered base skewed to crafters and small sellers, an in-house designer network, an existing 25%/20% affiliate program.
- Distribution surfaces ranked: Shopify App Store ($19 fee, 0% rev share to $1M, category proven), POD APIs (Printful/Printify post-merger, Gelato), Awin affiliate recruitment (Etsy's program lives there; craft creators are the publisher base), Amazon Selling Partner Appstore, Meta/TikTok partner badges once export-to-ads exists, Canva Apps (frenemy: decide policy).

## What this means for the three concepts

- Concept 1 (Kling co-launch) is timed exactly right: an imminent, plausibly September drop, an existing integration, a proven market pattern to copy, and a CEO who raised it himself. The pitch upgrade is "named in the announcement, keys two weeks early", and the deliverable is a reusable playbook, not one launch. It is also the safest Monday email: it uses only what Roemie said plus public patterns.
- Concept 2 (brand activation) should lead with proven buyers of exactly this behaviour (Crayola, Funko, Cricut) and the anti-AI-slop framing, which is CF's unique defensible angle (physical output). The Netflix precedent stays an internal reference: it is unverifiable publicly and its window has passed.
- Concept 3 (distribution) must stay a channel, not sales: zero-engineering moves first (affiliates via Awin, POD co-marketing, seller landing pages), the Shopify app flagged as the one engineering ask. It answers the CGO's "break from paid" problem with baseline lift and cohort-quality reporting, and it deliberately leaves API contracts to the Founding AE seat.
